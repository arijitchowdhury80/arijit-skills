## Rules

- The company is **Algolia**. Never "algolia", never "ALGOLIA", never an abbreviation.
- Product names take sentence case in running copy only where the brand itself does — `Agent Studio`,
  `Ask AI`, and `MCP Server` are proper nouns and always capitalized.
- Do not prefix every product with "Algolia". The pattern is "Algolia's Agent Studio" or just
  "Agent Studio", not "Algolia Agent Studio".
- Never invent a product name to fill a gap. If the capability has no marketed name, describe it.
