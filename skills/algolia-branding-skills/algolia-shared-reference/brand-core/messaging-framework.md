# Algolia messaging framework

`verified: 2026-08-05` · Source: live algolia.com, 2026 press boilerplate

## Positioning

Algolia is the **AI Search and Retrieval platform**. Not a search box, not a chatbot, not a
recommendation widget — the retrieval layer companies build discovery experiences on.

The differentiator is the **unified keyword and vector search and retrieval engine**: one engine
doing both, rather than a keyword system with semantics bolted on.

## Tagline

> **Agentic. Generative. Search.**
> One AI retrieval platform to power them all.

## Voice

Confident, clear, technically credible, approachable. Expert without condescension.

- Lead with what the reader gets, then how it works.
- Headlines are short and assertive. Subheads explain the mechanism in one sentence.
- Address the buyer as **"you / your customers"**. Use "we" sparingly.
- Product pages may use the imperative: "Show users what they're looking for."
- **Sentence case** everywhere except the brand name and product names.

## Voice — hard rules

**Anthropomorphism.** Algolia *enables, provides, powers, delivers*. It never *thinks, believes,
feels, wants,* or *cares*. This applies to the products too.

**Competitors.** Never named in customer-facing or marketing content — not Elasticsearch, Typesense,
Meilisearch, Coveo, Bloomreach, or Lucidworks. Say "legacy search", "traditional keyword-based
search", "their previous solution". Internal and sales-enablement documents may name them.

**No naked stats.** Every number carries its source. See `approved-stats.md`.

**Avoid:** hyperbole without a number, unsubstantiated superlatives ("best", "fastest", "only"),
hedging ("we think", "kind of", "might"), jokes, exclamation points, em-dash drama.

**Emoji:** not in marketing surfaces or product UI.

## Numbers

Big, abbreviated, and proud, always paired with a short noun phrase: `+112%` CVR boost, `4x`
conversion rate improvement, `100x` faster workflow. Customer results need that customer's approval
and get attributed to them.

## Editorial standards

AP Style · Oxford comma · sentence case headings · numbers under 10 spelled out · dates as
Month Day, Year · no double spaces · no periods on single-line bullets.

## Audience registers

| Audience | Lead with |
|---|---|
| Developers | Precision, working code, API accuracy. Never oversimplify. |
| Business and ecommerce leaders | Revenue, conversion, time-to-value. Never jargon-dump. |
| Executives | Outcome and proof in the first two sentences. Everything else is supporting. |
| Partners | The joint outcome. Neither brand subordinate. |

## CTAs

The canonical pair is **Get started** (primary, filled) + **Request demo** (secondary, outline).
One primary action per page or asset; every CTA on it points at the same next step.
