## Standard company description (~80 words)

The canonical block. Use at the foot of press releases, in partner pages, and anywhere a full
company description is required.

> Algolia is the leading AI Search and Retrieval platform, powering 1.75 trillion searches a year
> for more than 18,000 businesses. With a unified keyword and vector search and retrieval engine,
> Algolia delivers the world's fastest and most scalable search and discovery technology. Companies
> rely on Algolia to build agentic, generative, and search experiences through tools like Agent
> Studio. With over a decade of innovation, Algolia is redefining retrieval-powered applications
> and the future of AI discovery.

In HTML or web contexts, "Algolia" in the first sentence links to `https://www.algolia.com` and
"Agent Studio" links to `https://www.algolia.com/products/ai/agent-studio`.

## Rules

- **Verbatim only.** No trimming, no reordering, no "light editing for flow".
- Do not localize or translate without brand team sign-off.
- Do not append superlatives, awards, or customer names to these blocks. Analyst recognition goes in
  the body of the release, attributed, not in the boilerplate.
- If a vendor or partner requires an adapted version: *"Contact the Content Marketing Team for help
  adapting for specific vendors."*
- Never name a competitor in any of these.
