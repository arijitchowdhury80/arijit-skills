# Case study company inserts

`verified: 2026-08-05` · Numbers governed by `../brand-core/approved-stats.md`

The "about Algolia" block that closes a customer case study. Use verbatim. Two variants.

---

## Standard insert

> Algolia is the leading AI Search and Retrieval platform, powering 1.75 trillion searches a year
> for more than 18,000 businesses. With a unified keyword and vector search and retrieval engine,
> Algolia delivers the world's fastest and most scalable search and discovery technology. Companies
> rely on Algolia to build agentic, generative, and search experiences through tools like Agent
> Studio.

---

## Security-focused insert

For regulated industries — financial services, healthcare, government — and any customer whose
evaluation was driven by compliance.

> Algolia is the leading AI Search and Retrieval platform, powering 1.75 trillion searches a year
> for more than 18,000 businesses. With a unified keyword and vector search and retrieval engine,
> Algolia delivers the world's fastest and most scalable search and discovery technology.
>
> Algolia operates 70+ data centers across 17 regions, serving customers in more than 150 countries.
> Security and compliance posture, including current certifications and data residency options, is
> published at algolia.com — always link to the live page rather than restating certifications in
> collateral, which goes stale.

**Never list specific certifications, audit dates, or compliance frameworks in a case study.** Those
change, and a stale compliance claim is a legal problem. Link out.

---

## Structural notes

- The insert goes at the end, after the results section and CTA. It is boilerplate, not narrative.
- **The customer is the hero.** Algolia is the enabler. The insert is the only place the copy is
  about Algolia.
- Customer logo requires written permission. So does every metric.
- If the customer replaced a competitor, call it "their previous solution" or "their legacy search
  provider" — never the competitor's name. See `../brand-core/messaging-framework.md`.
- Quotes must be real and attributed with name, title, company. Never fabricate, never paraphrase a
  quote without marking it as paraphrase.
